Privacy Policy

Your data and your privacy.

This policy explains what information Over-Time processes and how it is used to provide the app.

Effective September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Over-Time collects, uses, stores, and processes information when you use the application.

01

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Over-Time, the application may process:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Account information
  • OJT attendance dates
  • Time-in and time-out records
  • Rendered internship hours
  • Daily accomplishments
  • Activity descriptions
  • Uploaded evidence or images
  • Application preferences
02

Authentication

User authentication is provided through Supabase Authentication.

Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not displayed to the developer during normal application use.

03

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide features including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Recording OJT attendance.
  • Calculating rendered internship hours.
  • Storing daily accomplishment records.
  • Displaying progress summaries and reports.
  • Storing evidence attached to logs.
  • Synchronizing account information across supported devices.
  • Maintaining and improving Over-Time.
04

Data Storage

Over-Time uses third-party infrastructure, including Supabase, to provide authentication, database functionality, and file storage.

The application may also use browser technologies such as caching, local storage, and service workers to support its Progressive Web App functionality.

05

Uploaded Evidence

Users may upload images or other supported evidence related to their OJT activities.

Before uploading

Avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive information. Make sure you have permission before uploading content containing other people or confidential workplace information.

06

Data Sharing

Over-Time does not intentionally sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the application, including authentication, hosting, database, and storage providers.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the application and its users.

07

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect user information.

However, no online service, database, or storage system can guarantee complete security.

Users should use strong passwords and should not share their account credentials.

08

Data Retention

Information may remain stored while an account is active or while it is reasonably necessary to provide Over-Time's features.

Data retention practices may change as the application continues to be developed.

09

Account and Data Requests

Users may contact the developer regarding questions about their account or information stored through Over-Time.

Available requests may depend on the functionality currently supported by the application.
